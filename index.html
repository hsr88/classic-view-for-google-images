A complete walkthrough of the layout controls and browsing experience.

Everything useful. Nothing in the way.

Classic horizontal rows

Replace the staggered masonry grid with clean, justified rows. Every thumbnail keeps its natural aspect ratio—without awkward vertical crops.

Hover image zoom

Preview a larger image the moment you hover. Smart positioning keeps the preview inside your viewport, even near the edges.

Direct View Image

Open the highest-resolution source available in a new tab, directly from the thumbnail. One click, no detour through the preview panel.

Quick size filters

Switch between All sizes, Large, Medium, and Icon using a compact filter bar placed directly above your results.

Less clutter

Hide Related searches blocks and carousels when you want more room for the images you actually searched for.

Your layout, your settings

Adjust row height from 120 to 300 pixels, tune tile spacing, and toggle every feature independently. Changes apply live and sync across devices.

Private by design

Your searches stay yours.

Classic View collects, stores, and transmits no user data or search history. It changes presentation through lightweight styling and preserves native Google behavior.
